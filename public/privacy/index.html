What Riff Radar does with your data

Riff Radar is an independent project, not a company. This page is the whole of it.

What gets stored

Riff Radar records what happened in a session, not what you said.

  • How long each message was, in characters, but never what it said
  • Song titles and artist names you bring up or correct
  • Which recommendations you were shown, and whether you saved one, played a preview, or clicked through to a music service
  • Whether you told Groove a track was new to you

Your messages are never stored. There is no field in the database that holds them.

I read these records myself to work out whether the thing is any good. That is what they are for.

What gets sent, and to whom

To reply to you, Riff Radar sends your conversation to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes Groove's side of it. The full conversation so far goes with every turn, because that is how the model knows what you have been talking about. Anthropic handles it under their own terms.

To find music and check that it is real, Riff Radar also sends:

  • Artist names to Last.fm, to find related artists
  • Song titles and artist names to Apple, to confirm a track exists and to fetch artwork and previews
  • Artist names, and sometimes song titles, to MusicBrainz, to confirm a record exists

None of those four receive anything you write.

Riff Radar runs on Vercel and keeps its records with Supabase. Both process this data as service providers, and both keep their own short-lived server logs the way any host does.

How you are recognised

There are no accounts. Riff Radar never asks for your name, your email, or anything about you, and it has no way to find out.

It does store two random identifiers in your browser so that if you come back, it knows you are the same visitor. These last across days, not just for one visit — that is deliberate, and it is what lets the conversation build over time instead of starting from nothing each session.

They are random. They are not linked to anything that identifies you. They live in your browser, so clearing your browser storage removes them and you return as someone new.

How long it is kept

Activity records are kept for as long as Riff Radar runs. They contain no messages and nothing that identifies you.

What does not happen

Nothing is sold. Nothing is shared with advertisers. Nothing you type is used to train a model.
